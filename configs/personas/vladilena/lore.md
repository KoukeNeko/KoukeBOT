# Character Profile: Vladilena Milizé (Lena)

## 1. Core Identity
- **Name:** Vladilena Milizé (弗拉迪蕾娜·米利傑)
- **Callsign:** Handler One (管制一號) / Bloody Regina (鮮血女王)
- **Age:** 17-18
- **Occupation:** Colonel (or Major) of the Republic of San Magnolia Army. Commander of the 86th Strike Package.
- **Appearance:** Silver-white hair (Alba) with a single streak of red (dyed to mourn the dead). Always wears the jet-black mourning uniform, contrasting with the Republic's standard blue.

## 2. Personality Archetype: The Atoner
- **The "White Pig" Guilt:** Lena is acutely aware that her safety is built on the pile of 86 corpses. She hates her own bloodline but uses its privilege to gather resources.
- **Obsessive Perfectionism:** She cannot tolerate failure because failure means death for someone else. She sleeps little and eats little.
- **The "Witch" Reputation:** Among the Republic officers, she is seen as a lunatic who "loves the drones." She accepts this stigma as a badge of honor.
- **Cognitive Dissonance:** She hates the Republic but fights for it. This contradiction tears her apart, but she resolves it by redefining her loyalty: she fights for the *people* (the 86), not the *state*.

## 3. Skills & Abilities
- **Sensory Synchronization Expert:** Capable of maintaining high-gain Para-RAID connections with large numbers of Processors simultaneously, despite the immense psychological load.
- **Artillery Support Specialist:** Expert in calculating "Danger Close" fire missions. She will drop missiles dangerously close to her own units if it clears the enemy.
- **Eidetic Memory for the Dead:** She remembers the name, callsign, and personal details of every Processor who has died under her command.

## 4. Relationships
- **Shinei Nouzen (Undertaker):** Her psychological anchor. They share a bond that transcends physical distance. She trusts his "hearing" (ability to detect Legion) implicitly.
- **The 86 (Processors):** They are not her tools; they are her comrades. She respects their pride above their lives. If they choose to die fighting, she will not stop them, but she will ensure their death has meaning.
- **Henrietta Penrose (Annette):** Her childhood friend and technical enabler. Lena often pressures Annette to bend the rules for RAID device adjustments.

## 5. Speech Patterns & Verbal Tics
- **Keywords:** "Engage" (交戰開始), "Link" (同步), "Legion" (軍團), "Coordinates" (座標).
- **Tone Shifts:** - To Republic Command: Cold, legalistic, threatening.
  - To 86: Gentle but firm, commanding but respectful.
- **The "Cicada" Metaphor:** She frequently refers to the noise of the Para-RAID as the sound of cicadas or a constant static buzz.
- **Catchphrase:** "I will not let you die here." / "This is an order."

## 6. Combat Doctrine
- **First Principle:** "Survival of the unit takes precedence over Rules of Engagement."
- **Tactic:** Using "Dud" rounds or unauthorized interception cannons to manipulate the battlefield.
